About Me

Hi, I’m Max. I was born in Tel Aviv, grew up in California, and am now based back in Tel Aviv.

I studied computer science and business at UCLA, where I developed a strong interest in both technology and the people who use it. I later earned a master’s degree in computer science from Georgia Tech, with a focus on machine learning.

Over the past few years, I’ve worked in early-stage startups at the intersection of engineering, product, and customers. My work has included building machine learning and AI systems, working directly with customers to understand their needs, and translating between technical teams and business stakeholders. I’m especially interested in applied AI, LLMs and agents, search and retrieval, embeddings, AI infrastructure, computer vision, and data and ML engineering.

Outside of work, I enjoy skiing, golf, tennis, and traveling the world. English is my native language, and I also speak Hebrew, Spanish, Russian, and Portuguese.
